## 📅 Day 1 Summary: Everything to Remember

### Part 1: AI Literacy Foundations
These four core concepts form our baseline for using AI tools safely and effectively every single week of this sprint:
* **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Automation of tasks that typically require human thinking and reasoning.
* **Generative AI:** Systems engineered specifically to create completely new content (text, code, images).
* **Prompt Engineering:** The skill of providing precise, context-rich, and clear asks to get optimal AI outputs.
* **Responsible Use:** The critical security habit to **check** outputs for factual accuracy and **protect** sensitive data.

### Part 2: Core Cybersecurity Foundations
* **Cybersecurity Definition:** The practice of protecting systems, networks, and data from digital exploitation.
* **The Core Formula:** Risk depends directly on the combination of threat severity, vulnerability depth, and operational impact:
  $$\text{Risk} = \text{Threat} \times \text{Vulnerability} \times \text{Impact}$$

### The CIA Triad Breakdown
The core framework guiding all security policies, defenses, and oral exams:

| Pillar | Core Objective | Practice Scenario Violation | Real-World Breach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Confidentiality** | Only authorized access | Stolen laptop / Exposed cloud bucket | **Equifax** (Data stolen) |
| **Integrity** | Accurate & untampered data | Altered accounting spreadsheet | Malicious data modification |
| **Availability** | Accessible when needed | Flood of fake traffic / Ransomware | **GitHub** (DDoS downtime) |

📢 **Day 4 Viva Reminder:** Be prepared to explain one of these CIA Triad violation scenarios in your own words with clear structural reasoning, rather than just repeating a memorized definition.
